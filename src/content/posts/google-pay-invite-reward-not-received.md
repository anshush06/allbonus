Google Pay rewards both people when someone new joins through an invite and makes their first payment. If your reward hasn't appeared, the cause is usually one of the points below. Most of them come straight from **Google's own help pages** for Google Pay India.

## How the invite reward is supposed to work

According to Google's help pages:

1. An existing user sends you an **invite link** (or code).
2. You join Google Pay **as a new user** using that link, with an Indian mobile number (**+91**).
3. You make your **first payment within 28 days** of signing up.
4. **Both of you** get a reward. It can take **up to 7 business days** to appear.

The reward amount depends on the offer running when you join, so check the invite or offer screen in the app.

## 1. It hasn't been 7 business days yet

This is the most common "problem". Google says the reward can take **up to 7 business days** after the qualifying payment. Weekends and bank holidays don't count.

**What to do:** note the date of your first payment and wait the full 7 business days before contacting support.

## 2. Your first payment was more than 28 days after sign-up

The qualifying payment must be made **within 28 days** of signing up. If you signed up and then didn't pay anyone for a month, the invite reward has expired for your account.

## 3. You're not a new Google Pay user

Invite rewards are only for people **new to Google Pay**. If you used Google Pay before, even years ago, with the same number or bank account, you don't count as new. This includes the old **Tez** app.

## 4. You didn't join through the invite link

The invite is recorded when you open the link or enter the code **while signing up**. If you installed Google Pay directly from the Play Store and set it up without the link or code, there's no invite to reward.

## 5. Your number isn't an Indian (+91) number

Google's page specifically says your friend needs an **Indian mobile number starting with +91**. Accounts on foreign numbers don't qualify for Indian invite rewards.

## 6. The payment didn't count

Some payments don't count as a **qualifying first payment**:

- sending money **to your own** bank account
- a failed or reversed payment
- a payment smaller than the minimum shown in the offer, if there is one

**What to do:** make a normal payment to a friend or a shop, then wait 7 business days.

## 7. Your inviter has reached their limit

Google limits how many invite rewards one person can earn. If your inviter has already earned the maximum, **their** reward may not come. Check the offer terms to see whether yours is affected too.

## 8. The reward is waiting in "Rewards"

Google Pay rewards often arrive as **scratch cards** in the Rewards section rather than as money in your bank.

**What to do:** open Google Pay, go to **Rewards** and check for unscratched cards.

## How to contact Google Pay support

If 7 business days have passed and everything above checks out:

1. Open Google Pay and tap your **profile photo**.
2. Tap **Help & feedback** (or *Get help*).
3. Search for "invite reward" or "referral" and choose the matching option.

Have these ready:

- your sign-up date
- the date and amount of your first payment
- the name or number of the person who invited you

## Set it up correctly from the start

If you haven't joined yet:

1. Open the **invite link** first. On our [Google Pay page](/payments/google-pay/), tap Download.
2. Install Google Pay and sign in with your **bank-linked +91 number** (the SIM must be in the phone).
3. Add your bank and **set your UPI PIN**. You can [use Aadhaar instead of a debit card](/blog/set-upi-pin-without-debit-card/).
4. Make your **first payment within 28 days**.
5. Wait **up to 7 business days** and check **Rewards**.

## Stay safe

Google Pay never asks for your **UPI PIN** to give you a reward, and you never need your PIN to **receive** money. If someone sends you a "collect request" saying you'll *get* cashback by entering your PIN, it's a scam. Decline it.
