You've signed up on Groww and finished KYC, but your account still says **verification in progress** or **activation pending**. Here's what normally happens, how long it takes, and what to do if it's stuck.

## How long Groww activation normally takes

Opening a stock market account in India involves more than Groww. Your details are checked and registered with a **KYC agency** and the **depository** (CDSL), which then creates your demat account. When everything matches, this usually takes about **24–48 hours** on working days.

| Account type | Typical time |
|---|---|
| Mutual funds only | Often within a day |
| Stocks (demat + trading) | Usually 1–2 working days |
| F&O (futures & options) | Only after the stocks account is active **and** you upload income proof |

Weekends and stock market holidays aren't working days, so an application made on Friday evening often activates on Monday or Tuesday.

## The most common reasons activation is delayed

### 1. Name mismatch
The name on your **PAN**, **Aadhaar** and **bank account** must match. Small differences are the number-one cause of delays, for example "Rahul Kumar" vs "Rahul K" or a missing middle name.

**Fix:** if Groww flags it, follow the in-app instructions. Sometimes you need to update your bank account name or Aadhaar details first.

### 2. PAN not linked with Aadhaar
An unlinked PAN is **inoperative**, and an inoperative PAN can't be used to open an investment account.

**Fix:** [check your PAN–Aadhaar link status](/blog/pan-aadhaar-link-status-check/) and link them if needed.

### 3. Signature rejected
Some applications need a signature photo. It's rejected if it's blurry, on lined paper, cut off, or doesn't match.

**Fix:** sign on **plain white paper** with a dark pen, take the photo in good light and re-upload it when asked.

### 4. Selfie or in-person verification failed
The face-match or "in-person verification" step fails in poor lighting, with sunglasses or a cap on, or if someone else appears in the frame.

**Fix:** retake it in daylight, facing the camera, with nothing covering your face.

### 5. Bank account verification failed
Groww verifies your bank account with a small test deposit or by checking the account number and IFSC. It fails for some account types, or when the account is not in your name.

**Fix:** use a **savings account in your own name** and double-check the IFSC.

### 6. E-sign not completed
The application has to be **e-signed with an Aadhaar OTP**. If you closed the app before entering the OTP, your form was never submitted.

**Fix:** open Groww. If it shows a pending e-sign step, complete it.

## What to do, step by step

1. **Open the Groww app** and look for a banner or notification. It usually says exactly what's missing.
2. **Check your email**, including spam, for messages from Groww about your documents.
3. **Wait two full working days** after the last step you completed.
4. **Still pending?** Go to *Profile → Help & Support* and raise a ticket about account activation. Mention the date you completed KYC.

## Does activation affect the referral reward?

Yes. Under Groww's referral programme, the **cash reward for both people is given after the referred account is activated**. Nothing is credited while your account is pending. Once you're activated, check **Refer & earn** in your profile. The referrer's own account must also be active.

## A word of caution

Activation is just the start. Investing in stocks and funds carries market risk, so begin with small amounts while you learn. Ignore anyone offering "guaranteed returns" or asking for your login OTP; that's a common scam.
