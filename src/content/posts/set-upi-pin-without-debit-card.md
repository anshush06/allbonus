To use UPI you need a **UPI PIN**. Most people set it with their **debit card** details, but if you don't have a debit card, or it's expired or blocked, you can set it with **Aadhaar OTP** instead. Most major UPI apps support this, including Google Pay and Paytm.

## What you need

Aadhaar-based UPI PIN setup only works if **all** of these are true:

| Requirement | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **The same mobile number** is linked to your **bank account** and your **Aadhaar** | You'll get one OTP from UIDAI (Aadhaar) and one from your bank on this number |
| Your **bank account is linked with Aadhaar** | The bank uses Aadhaar to confirm it's you |
| Your **bank supports Aadhaar OTP** for UPI PIN | Most large banks do; if yours doesn't, the option won't appear |
| The **SIM is in your phone** | UPI apps verify your number by SMS from this SIM |

If your bank number and Aadhaar number are different, update one of them first, at your bank branch or an Aadhaar centre.

## Step by step: Google Pay

1. Open Google Pay and tap your **profile photo**.
2. Tap **Bank accounts**, then the account you want to set up (or **Add bank account**).
3. Tap **Set UPI PIN**. When asked how to verify, choose **Aadhaar**.
4. Enter the **first 6 digits of your Aadhaar** number and agree to the consent.
5. Enter the **OTP from UIDAI**, then the **OTP from your bank**.
6. Create a new **4- or 6-digit UPI PIN** and confirm it.

## Step by step: Paytm

1. Open Paytm and go to **Profile → UPI & Payment Settings**.
2. Select your bank account and tap **Set / Reset UPI PIN**.
3. Choose **Aadhaar** as the verification method.
4. Enter the **first 6 digits of your Aadhaar** and give consent.
5. Enter the **two OTPs** (UIDAI and bank).
6. Set and confirm your new **UPI PIN**.

Other UPI apps follow the same pattern: *Bank account → Set UPI PIN → Aadhaar*.

## Common errors and fixes

**"Aadhaar option not available"**
Your bank doesn't support Aadhaar OTP for UPI yet, or your bank account isn't linked to Aadhaar. Ask your bank to link Aadhaar to your account, or use a debit card instead.

**"Mobile number not linked with Aadhaar"**
The number registered with your bank is different from the one on your Aadhaar. You must use one number for both. Update it at an Aadhaar Seva Kendra or your bank.

**OTP not received**
- Check that the **SIM is in this phone** and has signal.
- Wait a minute before requesting again, since repeated requests can be blocked for a while.
- Make sure your SMS inbox isn't full.

**"Aadhaar number mismatch"**
You entered the wrong digits, or the Aadhaar linked to your bank is different from the one you typed. Confirm with your bank which Aadhaar is linked.

**"Too many attempts"**
For security, UPI blocks PIN attempts for a while after several failures. Wait (usually 24 hours) and try again.

## Choosing a safe UPI PIN

- Don't use your birth year, `1234`, `0000` or your phone number's last digits.
- Use a **different PIN** from your ATM PIN if possible.
- **Never share your UPI PIN.** You only enter it to **send** money, never to receive it.

## Changing or resetting your PIN later

You can reset your UPI PIN anytime using the same Aadhaar method. Choose **Forgot UPI PIN** or **Reset UPI PIN** in your app and follow the same steps.

## Why this matters for referral rewards

Most referral offers on payment apps need you to **link your bank and make a first UPI payment**. If you can't set a PIN, you can't qualify. Once your PIN is set, follow the steps on our [Google Pay](/payments/google-pay/) or [Paytm](/payments/paytm/) pages to claim the new-user offer.
