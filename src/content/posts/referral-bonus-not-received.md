You signed up through a referral link, but no cashback, scratch card or reward showed up. This happens a lot, and it's almost always for one of the reasons below. Go through them in order. Most can be checked in a minute.

## 1. You installed the app before opening the referral link

This is the most common reason. A referral is recorded when the app is **installed or opened from the link**. If you installed the app from the Play Store first and opened the link afterwards, the app usually has no record that you were referred.

**Fix:** you can't go back and change it, but some apps let you enter a referral code during sign-up. If the app asks for one, enter it before you finish.

## 2. You're not a "new user"

Referral offers are for people who have **never used that app before**. Apps check this in several ways:

- your mobile number
- your PAN (for investing apps like Groww and Upstox)
- your bank account (for UPI apps)
- your phone (device ID)

If any of these has been used on the app before, even years ago or by a family member on the same phone, you may not count as new.

## 3. You didn't complete the qualifying action

Signing up alone rarely earns the reward. Each offer has a **qualifying action**, for example:

| App type | Typical qualifying action |
|---|---|
| UPI apps (Paytm, Google Pay) | First UPI payment, sometimes above a minimum amount |
| Investing apps (Groww, Upstox) | Account activation, sometimes a first trade or investment |
| Bill payment apps | First bill payment above a minimum amount |

**Fix:** open the offer inside the app and read exactly what counts. A payment to yourself, or between your own accounts, usually doesn't qualify.

## 4. KYC isn't finished

Investing apps can't credit anything until your **KYC is approved and the account is active**. UPI apps need your bank account linked and UPI PIN set. If your KYC is "under review", the reward is waiting on that.

**Fix:** check your KYC or account status in the app and upload anything it asks for. Mismatched names across PAN, Aadhaar and your bank are the usual cause of delays.

## 5. The offer had a time limit

Most offers must be completed within a set number of days after sign-up, often 7 to 30. After that the reward expires, even if you complete the action later.

## 6. The offer changed or ended

Apps change, pause and end referral programmes often. What counts is the offer **when you signed up**, as shown in the app, not an old screenshot or a post from months ago.

## 7. The reward was a scratch card you haven't opened

On UPI apps, rewards often arrive as a **scratch card** rather than cash. Look in *Rewards*, *Offers* or *Scratch cards*. They're easy to miss, and some expire if they aren't scratched.

## 8. Your account was flagged

Apps use automatic checks against referral abuse. Using an emulator, a VPN, multiple accounts on one phone, or someone else's bank account can block the reward or even the account.

**Fix:** always sign up with your own details on your own phone.

## 9. The reward is still processing

Some rewards take a few working days, especially on investing apps where the account has to be activated first.

**Fix:** wait 2–3 working days after completing the qualifying action before contacting support.

## 10. The app needs more information

Sometimes the reward is held because the app is waiting for something: email verification, a nominee, a bank account check or a signature upload.

**Fix:** open the app and look for pending tasks or notifications.

## How to contact support

If none of the above applies, contact the app directly. Only the app can check and credit referral rewards; websites like ours can't.

- **Paytm:** *Profile → Help & Support → Cashback & Offers*
- **Google Pay:** *Profile → Help & feedback*
- **Groww / Upstox:** *Help & Support* in the app, or the support page on their website

When you write to them, include your sign-up date, the referral link or code you used, and a screenshot of the offer if you have one.

## Stay safe

Nobody needs your **UPI PIN, OTP or password** to give you a reward. Anyone who asks for them, including people claiming to be from the app or from us, is a scammer. Real rewards appear inside the official app.
