**Groww** and **Upstox** are two of India's most popular apps for first-time investors. Both are **SEBI-registered stockbrokers**, both let you open an account online with PAN and Aadhaar, and both offer stocks, mutual funds and IPOs. The differences are in how they feel to use and who they're built for.

## Quick verdict

- **Choose Groww** if you want the **simplest** app, mainly for **mutual funds (SIPs)** and long-term stock investing.
- **Choose Upstox** if you want **more trading tools**, such as advanced charts and an option chain, and expect to trade more actively.
- **Or open both.** Many investors do, and each app has its own new-user referral reward.

**Charges** (brokerage, demat maintenance, and so on) change from time to time, so we don't list numbers here. Both apps show their current pricing in the app and on their websites. Compare them before you start, especially if you plan to trade often.

## Ease of use

**Groww** is known for its very **beginner-friendly** design. The home screen is simple, mutual funds are easy to browse and compare, and starting a SIP takes a few taps. If you're investing for the first time, it's one of the gentlest starts.

**Upstox** packs in **more features and data**. For simple buying and selling it's still straightforward, but its strengths, like charts, the option chain and market depth, are aimed at people who want to analyse and trade.

## Investments available

Both cover what most beginners need:

- **Stocks:** buy and hold (delivery) or intraday.
- **Mutual funds:** direct plans with no commission to the app; SIP or one-time.
- **ETFs and IPOs:** apply for IPOs using UPI.

Both also offer **futures & options**. SEBI studies have found that **most individual F&O traders lose money**, so beginners should avoid F&O until they truly understand it.

## Account opening

The process is almost identical on both, and takes about 15 minutes:

1. Sign up with mobile and email.
2. Enter PAN and complete **Aadhaar-based KYC**.
3. Add your bank account and take a selfie or short video.
4. **E-sign** with an Aadhaar OTP.
5. Activation usually follows within **1–2 working days**.

You'll need your **PAN linked with Aadhaar** for either app. [Check your link status here](/blog/pan-aadhaar-link-status-check/). Full steps are in our guide to [opening a demat account online](/blog/open-demat-account-online/).

## Referral rewards compared

- **Groww:** under its referral programme, **both** people get a **cash reward once the new account is activated**. The referrer's account must also be active.
- **Upstox:** gives **vouchers** (redeemable across many brands). Part comes when the account is **opened within 7 days** of signing up, and the rest after a **first trade within 30 days**.

Exact values depend on the campaign running when you join. Check each app's *Refer & earn* screen. Our [Groww page](/investing/groww/) shows how to claim step by step.

## Can you have both?

Yes. You can hold **more than one demat account** in your name. Some people use Groww for long-term mutual funds and Upstox for stock trading. Just remember each account may have its own yearly maintenance charges.

## Our take for beginners

If you're just starting and mainly want **SIPs and long-term investing**, **Groww's simplicity** is hard to beat. If you're curious about **charts and active trading**, **Upstox** gives you room to grow. Either way, start small, invest regularly, and never invest money you might need in the short term.
