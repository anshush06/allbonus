**UPI Lite** is a small balance stored in your UPI app that lets you make **small payments without entering your UPI PIN**. It was launched by NPCI, the organisation that runs UPI, to make everyday payments like tea, auto fares and groceries faster and more reliable.

## UPI Lite in one minute

- You **load money** into UPI Lite from your bank account (this step uses your UPI PIN).
- After that, you pay small amounts from the UPI Lite balance **without a PIN**.
- Payments are **faster** because they don't hit your bank's servers each time.
- Your **bank statement stays clean**: you see one top-up entry instead of dozens of ₹20 payments. The small payments are listed in the app instead.

## UPI Lite limits

Under the limits NPCI announced in **February 2025**:

| Limit | Amount |
|---|---|
| Maximum per payment | **₹1,000** |
| Maximum UPI Lite balance at any time | **₹5,000** |

Before that, the limits were ₹500 per payment and ₹2,000 balance, and some older articles still quote them. Limits can change, so check the UPI Lite screen in your app for what applies to you.

For payments above ₹1,000, you simply use normal UPI with your PIN.

## How to turn on UPI Lite

The steps are similar in all major apps.

**On Google Pay**
1. Tap your **profile photo** and choose **UPI Lite**.
2. Pick a bank account that supports UPI Lite.
3. Enter an amount to add, up to the balance limit, and confirm with your **UPI PIN**.

**On Paytm**
1. Search for **UPI Lite** in the app, or find it on the home screen.
2. Choose your bank account, enter an amount and confirm with your **UPI PIN**.

Not every bank supports UPI Lite yet. If yours isn't listed, the option won't appear for that account.

## How to pay with UPI Lite

Scan a QR code or choose a contact as usual. If the amount is within the UPI Lite limit and you have enough balance, the app pays from UPI Lite **without asking for your PIN**. Some apps let you choose between UPI Lite and your bank account at the payment screen.

## Auto top-up

Many apps let you turn on **auto top-up**. When your UPI Lite balance falls below an amount you choose, it refills automatically from your bank account, so you don't run out at the shop counter.

## Is UPI Lite safe?

UPI Lite is part of the official UPI system, but because it doesn't ask for a PIN, **anyone holding your unlocked phone could spend the Lite balance**. That's why the limits are low.

To stay safe:

- **Lock your phone** with a PIN, pattern or fingerprint, and lock the payment app too.
- Keep only what you need for daily spending in UPI Lite.
- If you lose your phone, **disable UPI Lite** from the app on a new device or call your bank. You can withdraw the remaining balance back to your bank account from the UPI Lite settings.

## Pros and cons

| Pros | Cons |
|---|---|
| No PIN for small payments | Spendable by anyone with your unlocked phone |
| Faster, more reliable at busy times | Low limits (₹1,000 per payment) |
| Cleaner bank statement | Not every bank supports it |
| Auto top-up available | Balance doesn't earn interest |

## Can UPI Lite earn referral rewards?

Payments from UPI Lite are UPI payments, but referral offers often say exactly which payment qualifies. If you're new to an app, make your **first qualifying payment** the way the offer describes, usually a normal UPI payment. ## Quick answers

**Do I need a PIN to add money to UPI Lite?** Yes. Topping up uses your normal UPI PIN.

**Can I receive money into UPI Lite?** In most apps, incoming UPI money goes to your bank account, not to UPI Lite. Check your app's UPI Lite screen.

**Can I get my UPI Lite balance back?** Yes. You can move it back to your bank account from the UPI Lite settings, or by disabling UPI Lite.
