Paytm's **Refer & Win** programme rewards both people when a new user joins through a referral and makes a qualifying payment. When the reward doesn't show up, it's almost always for one of the reasons below. Work through them in order.

**Quick check first:** open Paytm, search for **Refer & Win** in the app, and look at the offer screen. It shows the current reward, the qualifying payment and the time limit. Compare what you did with what it asks for.

## 1. You weren't a first-time Paytm user

Paytm's rules say the person being referred must be a **first-time user**. Paytm checks more than your phone number. If any of these were used on Paytm before, you usually won't qualify:

- your mobile number, even on an old account you've forgotten about
- your bank account
- your phone (device), including one you've reset

**What to do:** nothing can change this. If you had a Paytm account years ago, you're an existing user for this offer.

## 2. Paytm was installed before you opened the referral link

A referral is recorded when you **open the referral link first** and then install or sign up. If you installed Paytm from the Play Store, then opened your friend's link afterwards, Paytm has no record of the referral.

**What to do:** if the app asks for a **referral code** during sign-up, enter it there. If it doesn't ask, the referral can't be added later.

## 3. Registration isn't complete

Signing up with your number is only the first part. Paytm counts you as registered only after you verify your number with the OTP and **link your bank account for UPI**, which means setting a UPI PIN.

**What to do:** go to *Profile → UPI & Payment Settings* and check that your bank account shows as linked. If you can't set a PIN, see [how to set a UPI PIN without a debit card](/blog/set-upi-pin-without-debit-card/).

## 4. Your first payment didn't qualify

The reward needs a **qualifying transaction**, usually your first UPI payment. Common reasons a payment doesn't count:

- it was **below the minimum amount** shown in the offer
- it was a transfer **to your own account** or to yourself
- it was made **before** your registration was complete
- it was a **recharge or bill payment** when the offer asked for a UPI money transfer, or the other way round

**What to do:** read the offer screen again and make one payment that exactly matches it, to a friend or a shop.

## 5. The time limit passed

Most referral offers must be completed within a fixed number of days after sign-up. If you made the first payment after that window, the offer has expired for your account.

## 6. The reward is a scratch card you haven't opened

Paytm often gives rewards as **scratch cards** or **cashback offers**, not direct cash. They're easy to miss.

**What to do:** open *Cashback & Offers* (or search "scratch card" in the app) and look for unopened cards. Some cards expire if you don't scratch them in time.

## 7. Your referrer isn't eligible

The **person who referred you** must also meet Paytm's rules. They must be an active user aged **18 or over** with a complete account. Their monthly referral limit may also have been reached. This affects their reward, and sometimes the campaign as a whole.

## 8. The account was flagged by fraud checks

Paytm automatically checks for referral abuse. Rewards can be blocked if it detects:

- several accounts on one phone
- an emulator, VPN or app-cloning tools
- someone else's bank account linked to your Paytm

**What to do:** always use your own phone, number and bank account.

## Still not received? Contact Paytm

Only Paytm can check and credit referral rewards. Websites like ours can't add them.

1. Open Paytm and go to *Profile → Help & Support*.
2. Choose **Cashback & Offers** (or *Rewards*).
3. Pick the referral offer and describe the problem. Include your sign-up date and the date and amount of your first payment.

## Before you start: do it right the first time

If you haven't joined yet, here's the order that works:

1. Open the **referral link** first. On our [Paytm page](/payments/paytm/), tap the Download button.
2. Install Paytm **from that link**.
3. Sign up with your **own bank-linked mobile number**.
4. Link your bank and **set your UPI PIN**.
5. Make the **qualifying payment** shown in the offer, within the time limit.
6. Check **Cashback & Offers** for your reward.

## Stay safe

Nobody needs your **UPI PIN or OTP** to give you cashback. You never enter your PIN to *receive* money. Anyone asking for these, including callers claiming to be from Paytm, is trying to scam you.
