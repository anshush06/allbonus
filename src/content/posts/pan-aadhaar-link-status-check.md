Your **PAN must be linked with your Aadhaar**. If it isn't, the PAN becomes **inoperative**, and you can't use it to file income tax returns, get refunds, or open a demat or mutual fund account. Here's how to check your status for free and what to do if it isn't linked.

## Check your PAN–Aadhaar link status (no login needed)

1. Go to the Income Tax e-filing portal: **incometax.gov.in**.
2. On the home page, find **Quick Links** and click **Link Aadhaar Status**.
3. Enter your **PAN** and your **Aadhaar number**.
4. Click **View Link Aadhaar Status**.

The check is **free** and you don't need an account on the portal.

## What the result means

| Message | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Already linked** | Your PAN and Aadhaar are linked | Nothing. You're done. |
| **Linking request pending / sent for validation** | You've applied and it's being processed | Wait a few working days and check again |
| **Not linked** | They aren't linked; your PAN may be inoperative | Link them (see below) |

## How to link PAN with Aadhaar

For most people the free linking deadline ended on **30 June 2023**. Linking after that needs a **late fee of ₹1,000** under **Section 234H** of the Income Tax Act. You pay the fee **first**, then submit the linking request.

### Step 1: Pay the ₹1,000 fee
1. On incometax.gov.in, use **e-Pay Tax** (no login needed for this option).
2. Enter your PAN and mobile number and verify the OTP.
3. Choose **Income Tax** and the **current assessment year**.
4. Select type of payment **Other Receipts (500)** and enter ₹1,000 under "Others".
5. Pay through net banking, UPI, or a card, depending on the options shown.

### Step 2: Submit the link request
1. Wait until the payment is reflected, which can take a few working days.
2. On the portal home page, go to **Quick Links → Link Aadhaar**.
3. Enter PAN and Aadhaar, confirm your name as on Aadhaar and your mobile number, and verify the OTP.
4. Submit, then check the status after a few days using the steps above.

## Why linking fails

- **Name mismatch** between PAN and Aadhaar, e.g. initials vs full name.
- **Date of birth** differs between the two.
- **Gender** recorded differently.

If any of these differ, **correct the details first**, either the PAN (through the PAN correction service) or Aadhaar (through UIDAI), then try linking again.

## What an inoperative PAN means for you

While your PAN is inoperative:

- You **can't file** income tax returns normally, and pending **refunds** aren't issued.
- TDS and TCS may be deducted at **higher rates**.
- You'll face problems with **banks, mutual funds and stock brokers**, and new demat accounts can't be opened.

After you link (with the fee), your PAN becomes **operative again**. This can take up to about **30 days**.

## Why this matters for investing apps

Brokers like Groww and Upstox **can't activate your account** with an inoperative PAN. If your [Groww account activation is pending](/blog/groww-account-activation-pending/), check your PAN–Aadhaar link first. It's one of the most common hidden causes.

## Watch out for scams

Fake messages saying *"Your PAN will be blocked today, click here"* are common. The only official site is **incometax.gov.in**. Never enter your PAN, Aadhaar or bank details on a link from SMS or WhatsApp.

*Rules and fees can change. Check the latest notices on incometax.gov.in before paying.*
