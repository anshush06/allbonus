You paid ₹500 at a shop, the app says "failed", and your bank SMS says ₹500 is gone. Don't pay again yet. Under RBI's rules, a **failed UPI transfer to a person should be reversed by the next day (T+1)**, and a **failed payment to a merchant within 5 days (T+5)**. If the bank is late, it owes you **₹100 for every extra day**.

Here "T" is the day you made the payment, and RBI counts calendar days, so Sundays and bank holidays count.

## How long does a failed UPI payment refund take?

RBI fixed these timelines in a circular on failed transactions dated **20 September 2019** (RBI/2019-20/67). The two UPI cases are:

| What happened | Bank must reverse by | Compensation if late |
|---|---|---|
| Money debited, but the **other person's account was not credited** | **T + 1** | ₹100 per day of delay |
| Money debited, but the **merchant never got confirmation** | **T + 5** | ₹100 per day of delay |

The refund goes back to the account you paid from, not to your app wallet. Those deadlines are what you can hold the bank to.

## What should I do right after a UPI payment fails?

1. **Check the status in the app.** Open the payment history. "Pending" or "processing" is not the same as "failed". Many pending payments settle on their own.
2. **Check your bank SMS or passbook.** If there is a debit but no matching credit-back, note the amount, date and **UTR / transaction ID**.

   Only use the help option inside the app, or the number on your bank's official website or the back of your card. Fake "UPI refund helpline" numbers on Google and social media are a common scam. A genuine refund never needs your UPI PIN, an OTP, or a screen-sharing app.
3. **Screenshot everything**: the failure screen, the debit SMS and the transaction ID.
4. **Wait for the deadline.** For a person-to-person transfer, wait until the next day. For a shop payment, give it up to 5 days.
5. **Don't pay a second time** until you know whether the first went through, especially at a merchant. Ask them if they got the money.

## Money still not back? Where do I complain?

Go in this order. The RBI Ombudsman won't accept your complaint unless you complained to the bank first and can show proof.

1. **The payment app.** Paytm and Google Pay both have a help or "raise an issue" option on the payment itself. RBI requires UPI apps to let you lodge disputes inside the same app you paid with. Many apps also have a **UPI Help** option in the transaction details. It's NPCI's system, and it sends your complaint to your bank, so it's another way in, not a separate escalation.
2. **Your bank.** Call customer care or visit the branch with your transaction ID and screenshots, and ask for a written complaint number. If the deadline has passed, ask why the ₹100-a-day compensation hasn't been credited.
3. **RBI Ombudsman.** If the bank doesn't reply within **30 days**, or you aren't happy with the answer, file at **cms.rbi.org.in**. Under RBI's Integrated Ombudsman Scheme 2026 (in force from 1 July 2026), you have **90 days** from the end of that 30-day window, or from the bank's last reply, whichever is later. Filing is free.

## Why do UPI payments fail after debit?

Usually it's a technical hiccup between banks, not your fault. Common causes are a slow network at your bank or the receiver's bank, the app closing before the confirmation arrives, or a merchant system timing out. The money is held in between and returned automatically.

If the failure is on your side, like a wrong PIN or low balance, the money is normally never debited at all.

## FAQ

**Is my money safe if a UPI payment fails?**
Yes, in the sense that the bank must return it. The timeline is T+1 or T+5 depending on the type of payment.

**Do I get compensation for a late refund?**
RBI's circular sets ₹100 for each day of delay past the deadline, and says the bank should credit it without waiting for you to complain. If it hasn't arrived, ask for it in your complaint.

**Can I get a refund if I sent money to the wrong person?**
That is different from a failed payment. The payment succeeded, so there's no automatic reversal. Tell your bank immediately and ask the receiver to return it.

**Do weekends or bank holidays add time?**
No. RBI's circular counts calendar days from the day of the payment.

**Does it cost anything to complain to the RBI Ombudsman?**
No. RBI says there is no fee for filing or resolving complaints.
